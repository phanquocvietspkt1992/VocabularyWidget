# Vocabulary Widget

A small native iPhone app and Home Screen widget, kept separate from the Unity game. The app lets the learner choose one topic. The widget displays the current word, Vietnamese meaning, abbreviated part of speech, example sentence, and position in the topic. The catalog has five IT/work topics plus the three earlier general topics, with 50 entries per topic. Built-in topic names are displayed in English, and the new-install default is **Work Communication**. The lists are curated for learner usefulness and are not a strict frequency ranking or yet linked to the Unity game's vocabulary bank. Progress is stored per topic in an App Group shared defaults suite. Tapping **Mark as learned** also appends a versioned study-history record containing the topic ID, word text, local `yyyy-MM-dd` day, and tap time. Records are retained, deduplicated per topic/word/local Gregorian day, and kept when progress is reset. Concurrent **Mark as learned** taps from different processes may lose a history record; this is an accepted limitation of shared defaults without cross-process locking.

For the product decisions and context carried over from the original ChatGPT conversation, see [`CONVERSATION_HANDOFF.md`](CONVERSATION_HANDOFF.md).

## Open in Xcode

Long-press a custom topic and choose **Delete Topic** to remove it after confirmation. This also permanently removes its custom words, progress, study history and review data. Built-in topics cannot be deleted.

Open `VocabularyWidget.xcodeproj` and run the `VocabularyWidgetApp` scheme on an iPhone simulator or device. In the app, choose a topic; on the Home Screen, add **Vocabulary Widget** from the widget gallery. The topic list shows the active word and an approximate time until it changes. Long-press a topic row and choose **Restart Progress** to reset that topic after confirmation. The medium widget has **Next word** and **Mark as learned** buttons; the small widget has matching arrow and checkmark buttons. **Next word** advances without logging study. **Mark as learned** records the displayed word and advances, restarting its five-minute interval. Both widget sizes also have an icon-only speaker beside the displayed word. It pronounces only that word (not its meaning/example), preferring an en-US voice and otherwise an available English voice, at 90% of the default speech rate. Playback works in silent mode, mixes with other audio without ducking it, and does not change progress or history. Pronunciation is rendered to PCM buffers and played by an in-process audio engine. A repeated tap replaces the current pronunciation without releasing the audio session; same-word taps within 300 ms are ignored. The audio session remains active for the process lifetime after its first activation, and is reactivated after an interruption ends or media services reset. Playback stops the player and pauses the engine between requests; the engine starts again for the next request. Rendering is bounded to five seconds, playback to 30 seconds, and a `cannotStartPlaying` activation failure is retried once after 150 ms. If activation is denied while the app is backgrounded, that is recorded in the pronunciation diagnostics. Route-driven engine configuration changes restart buffered playback; a media-services reset recreates the renderer and engine for the next request. The shared iOS 17 `AudioPlaybackIntent` runs in the app process. Only the host app declares the audio background mode so widget-triggered pronunciation can activate its audio session while the app is backgrounded; the widget extension does not. The vocabulary is bundled locally in `Shared/VocabularyData.swift`.

For the repeatable physical-device signing/install workflow, see [the iPhone device build skill](.agents/skills/iphone-device-build/SKILL.md).

For changes driven by user feedback, see [the PM → BA → Dev → DevOps → QC workflow](.agents/skills/feature-feedback-workflow/SKILL.md).

The app identifier is `com.phanquocviet.VocabularyWidget`, the extension identifier is `com.phanquocviet.VocabularyWidget.WidgetKit`, and the App Group is `group.com.phanquocviet.VocabularyWidget` (team `PD2729ZJNT`). For updates, keep these identities and both App Group entitlements unchanged and install over the existing app. Do not uninstall, delete app data, or reset the App Group defaults before deploying an update. Changing identity is not an in-place update and needs a separately designed migration; the app does not recover an inaccessible previous App Group or data removed by uninstalling.

Same-identity updates retain custom topic IDs, names and order; custom words and all five examples (including additions to built-in topics); selection, per-topic progress, anchors, study history and schedule preferences. Built-in catalog content is merged with, not written over, custom storage. If custom storage or a saved schedule cannot be decoded, reads may show empty data or default settings, but additions/settings writes reject the unreadable payload rather than replacing it. Startup also preserves an unresolved saved topic selection when its custom catalog is unreadable. This prevents further loss; it does not repair corrupt data.

The timeline schedules words five minutes apart in advance. WidgetKit controls actual refresh timing and may display an entry later than its scheduled time, so an exact five-minute cadence cannot be guaranteed by iOS. The app refreshes the widget when the selected topic changes and when the app returns to the foreground.

## Sentence Game

Open the **Sentence Game** tab to create and play sentence topics. It is separate from **Vocabulary**: sentence pairs, rounds and narrator controls do not use vocabulary topics or progress. The widget remains vocabulary-only. Enter each pair as an English sentence on one line and its Vietnamese meaning on the next; blank lines may separate pairs. Existing tab-separated rows remain supported. Exact repeated pairs are skipped; conflicting English/Vietnamese mappings report their source lines without silently choosing one. Four unique pairs unlock play.

**Stage** asks every pair once in shuffled order and uses selection plus confirmation. Selecting an option reads its letter and English answer, then asks “Final answer?”; scoring waits for **Confirm Answer**. **Quick** asks up to five questions and scores immediately, speaking the selected answer before the result. Both automatically read the currently displayed English choices A–D in their shuffled order; the Vietnamese prompt stays visible as text and is not spoken. The **Audio** menu offers installed English voices, narrator, music and sound-effect controls. The game keeps its original synthesized sounds and visuals; it does not use show recordings, music, or branding. The primary Confirm/Next action stays pinned at the bottom while the question content can scroll when needed. **Answer History** in the Sentence Game root keeps every committed answer, including retries, in newest-first order.

Each topic and mode has a separate saved round. Reopen it with **Resume Round** to restore the topic snapshot, shuffled questions and choices, selection, and committed answers; completed rounds remain available as **View Results**. **New Game** asks before replacing an unfinished round, while **Play Again** replaces a completed round. Topic deletion removes that topic's saved rounds only after the topic deletion succeeds.

Sentence topics use the host's `sentenceTopics.v1` defaults key, answer history uses `sentenceRoundHistory.v1`, and resumable rounds use `sentenceGameSessions.v1`. All remain separate from the vocabulary App Group and backup. Corrupt data refuses replacement, and detected write/readback failures restore previous bytes and report the failure; failed history writes can be retried and are deduplicated per committed round question. UserDefaults verification and rollback are not crash-safe disk transactions. Word links wait until the game sheet/tab routing permits them.

Run `sh Tests/run_sentence_game_checks.sh` for isolated topic/history/session persistence, exact round restoration, rollback/corruption behavior, import validation, question/scoring/retry rules, English-only speech plans, installed-voice validation, stale-generation cancellation and music/narration gating. Build or launch evidence alone does not verify audible output, in-game layout, or navigation interactions.

## Vocabulary Topic Details

Tap a topic in the **Vocabulary** tab to open its word list. Each word has a pronunciation button; expand **Examples** to hear any example. Opening a word shows **Word Detail**, where every example can also be played individually. Vocabulary playback is independent of the Sentence Game narrator. The widget continues to use vocabulary data only.

## Word Detail

Tap only the word text on the small or medium widget to open **Word Detail**; tapping the rectangular Lock Screen widget opens its displayed word. Existing widget speaker, Next and Mark buttons remain App Intents. Empty widgets link to the topic list.

Detail shows the topic, word, part of speech, Vietnamese meaning and all five examples. It keeps the tapped word while the cadence advances. Opening or closing does not itself select a topic, change progress/history/anchors, or autoplay. **Speak** is explicit foreground playback, canceled on closing, another detail link, progress action, or an inactive scene. This feature does not fix or make new promises about background widget-speaker audio.

**Next word** and **Mark as learned** work only for the currently selected topic, advancing from the displayed index and restarting the interval at the action time. Mark uses the existing topic/word/local-day deduplication. Rejected callbacks keep the displayed word and do not trigger widget/reminder refreshes; the existing shared-defaults operations are not disk-atomic transactions. Another topic remains readable without automatically selecting it.

Links use `vocabularywidget-word://word/detail?v=1&topicID=…&index=…&text=…` with URLComponents encoding; the root is `vocabularywidget-word://app/`. Invalid routes are ignored. Missing/deleted/reordered words or unreadable catalogs show unavailable content without searching for a replacement. Each accepted tap gets a fresh ephemeral detail; links received during another sheet or confirmation wait until it finishes, retaining only the latest link. Nothing about this queue is persisted.

The progress runner also checks strict URLs, exact tuple resolution, failures with unchanged state, raw widget-action equivalence, routing and the production playback controller with an injected performer. Native cold/warm URL dispatch, actual widget gestures, modal timing, Dynamic Type/VoiceOver layout and audible playback remain DevOps/QC checks; an SDK typecheck is not runtime verification.

## Backup and Daily Review

Choose **More → Backup & Restore** to export a JSON file or import one from Files. Import validates the whole backup and current storage before offering **Replace**; Cancel changes nothing. Replacement restores all app-owned saved keys, removes owned keys absent from the backup, and leaves unrelated preferences alone. It does not request notification permission. Widgets and already-authorized reminders refresh only after a successful restore.

Schema 1 contains a creation date and a base64-encoded binary property-list snapshot. The snapshot preserves raw stored JSON bytes, types, optional-key absences, custom topic/word order, additions to built-in topics, selection, progress/anchors/visited flags, study history, schedule, and review records/attempts. Bundled vocabulary and OS permissions are not included. An empty saved state can be backed up; an empty file or `{}` is not a backup. Unreadable existing stores block both export and restore rather than being silently repaired. Keep exported files private: they contain your saved vocabulary and learning history.

Choose **Daily Review** for the selected topic. Each session contains up to ten distinct words: overdue words first (oldest due date, then catalog order), followed by unrated words. Reveal shows the meaning and examples and enables **Remember** / **Again**. Remember schedules the next review after 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 local calendar days, capped at 30; Again resets the stage and schedules tomorrow. Due dates use local midnight, not fixed 24-hour intervals. **Reviewed Today** counts distinct existing word identities in the selected topic, including Again attempts, using timestamps in the current local time zone. **Due** is for the selected topic. Review results are separate from widget study history; grading does not change widget selection, progress, cadence, history, or reminders.

Closing and reopening review creates a fresh queue. Topic/catalog/restore changes, newly due dates, midnight, and foreground refresh invalidate stale cards as needed. Missing topics or removed words are ignored at runtime but retained in backups; an undeclared custom-topic reference is rejected during validation.

Writes are checked by readback. On a detected failure, previous data is restored and the actual error is shown; a failed review grade retains its revealed card and counters. If rollback also fails, **Export Recovery Backup** exposes the previous snapshot while that screen remains open. Shared UserDefaults does not provide crash-safe transactions or cross-process atomicity: a concurrent widget write can race with replacement, and process termination or an OS persistence failure cannot be guaranteed recoverable. Keep a backup before replacing data.
